HAMU MASTER V27 — SESSION REPLACED WARNING

When the same account logs in from another browser/device:
- The previous session is revoked immediately.
- Active gameplay is paused and pointer lock is released.
- A compact warning overlay appears: ⚠️ ANOTHER PLAYER HAS LOGGED IN TO YOUR ACCOUNT.
- The overlay shows a live 5-second countdown and animated timer bar.
- After 5 seconds the old browser returns to the login screen.
