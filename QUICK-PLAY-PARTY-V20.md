# HAMU MASTER V20 — Quick Play Party Lobby

- Removed the Play With Friends navigation/page from the main player flow.
- Quick Play is now the single squad lobby: up to 4 party members.
- Friends can be invited directly from the Quick Play right rail.
- Incoming party invitations can be accepted/declined from Quick Play.
- The inviter remains party leader; invited players are non-leaders and can only Ready/Unready.
- Only the leader can change the shared playlist/mode.
- Starting a match queues the complete ready party together.
- Party member operator + equipped loadout are synchronized to all party members and shown by the 3D lobby characters.
- Each visible lobby character has a blue layered circular footing/glow.
- FFA remains an 8-player match, but a single party is capped at 4 members.
- Previous account/auth/persistence/admin systems are retained.
