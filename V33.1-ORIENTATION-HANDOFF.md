# HAMU MASTER V33.1 — AUG/ASSAULT + BULLPUP orientation fix

This is the complete V33 game with the owner's requested two-weapon correction, not a fully release-validated build. Read this note before V33-HANDOFF.md; the original handoff remains applicable outside this narrow change.

## Authorized change

Only the ASSAULT/AUG visual (`ar4`, `assault.glb`) and BULLPUP visual (`hxr8`, `bullp.glb`) were corrected in `client/weapon-models.js`:
- ASSAULT source barrel points +Z; preparation now rotates it by 180 degrees around Y to game-forward -Z.
- BULLPUP source barrel points +X; preparation now uses +90 rather than -90 degrees around Y.
- Repositioned these two models and adjusted their grip/muzzle anchors to their correctly oriented source geometry. BULLPUP's ADS eye line is raised above its upper rail/stock so the reversed stock no longer blocks camera centre.
- Shared preparation applies to first-person, lobby, NPC and remote mounts. No extra gun bodies, geometry or skins were fabricated.

The other nine weapon definitions, all original GLBs/PNGs, combat values, characters, maps, UI, accounts, multiplayer and unrelated systems are unchanged. Old preview-framing/UI-label defects were not fixed in this request.
